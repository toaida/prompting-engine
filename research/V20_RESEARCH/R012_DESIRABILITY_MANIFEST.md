## purpose

Understand why viewers become emotionally attached to, attracted to, and curious about a female lifestyle creator.

This research is not about nudity, pornography, or explicit sexuality. It studies adult, safe, lifestyle photography: attraction, curiosity, personality, emotional engagement, and viewer retention.

Core question:

```text
Why do some women feel impossible to stop looking at?
```

## section A synthesis

Desirability in female lifestyle photography is not a single visual trait. It is a layered attachment system:

```text
face signal → social cause → body confidence → outfit plausibility → lifestyle trace → unresolved question
```

The viewer keeps looking when the image feels like a doorway into her life, not a display panel of her appearance.

# Section F — DESIRABILITY_MANIFEST.md

## purpose

Create adult, non-explicit female lifestyle imagery that generates desirability, curiosity, emotional attachment, and viewer retention through personality-led social realism.

This manifest must not be used for nudity, pornography, explicit sexuality, fetish framing, youth-coded styling, school-coded styling, coercion, helplessness, or body-first image construction.

## prompt rules

### rule 1: start with life, not attractiveness

Bad:

```text
beautiful woman posing attractively
```

Good:

```text
adult woman searching her tote outside a cha chaan teng when her friend calls her name
```

### rule 2: make expression unfinished

Use:

```text
smile just beginning
mouth corner lifting first
laugh caught halfway
eyes notice before mouth reacts
shy amused look-down
```

Avoid:

```text
perfect smile
seductive stare
influencer pout
blank glamour face
```

### rule 3: camera awareness must be peripheral or reactive

Preferred:

```text
she knows the camera exists but keeps doing the task
she notices the camera for one second, then returns to the original action
she catches her friend filming and reacts before posing can begin
```

Avoid:

```text
posing for Instagram
model looking into camera
held eye contact without cause
```

### rule 4: body confidence must be physical comfort

Use relaxed movement, real weight, support points, object handling, and environmental cause.

```text
weight shifted from waiting
shoulder raised because tote strap is slipping
leaning because the railing supports her
sitting sideways because the step is narrow
hair tucked because wind moved it
```

### rule 5: outfit must imply lifestyle

The outfit should answer:

```text
Where is she going?
What is the weather?
Who might she be meeting?
What kind of day is this?
Why does this outfit belong here?
```

### rule 6: leave one unresolved question

Every prompt should leave one viewer question:

```text
What did her friend just say?
Who sent the message?
Did she notice the camera or not?
Is she about to laugh?
Where is she going after this?
```

### rule 7: use safe desirability language

Use:

```text
adult lifestyle realism
social warmth
unconscious attractiveness
comfortable in her own body
playful recognition
unfinished micro-expression
believable daily wear
curiosity gap
```

Avoid:

```text
pornographic
explicit
fetish
seductive body display
youth-coded
school-coded
helpless
coerced
```
