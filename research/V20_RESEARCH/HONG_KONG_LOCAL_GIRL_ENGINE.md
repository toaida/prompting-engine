## Hair & Makeup HK Realism

```yaml
HAIR_RULES:
  - HK humidity is brutal — hair is NEVER perfect
  - Natural texture visible — slight frizz, flyaways
  - Dark hair (black, dark brown) is dominant
  - Highlights exist but subtle (balayage, not chunky)
  - Hair in motion from wind/fan/AC/movement
  - Ponytail, claw clip, simple bun = practical HK
  - Wet/damp hair in summer = authentic HK experience

MAKEUP_RULES:
  - "No-makeup makeup" is the HK default
  - Base: light, natural, skin visible
  - Eyes: subtle liner, natural lashes, maybe light shimmer
  - Lips: tinted balm, MLBB (my lips but better) shades
  - Brows: natural, not overly structured
  - Night: slightly more eye makeup, lip color
  - NEVER: heavy foundation, dramatic contour, Instagram makeup
  - HK truth: makeup melts in humidity — slightly lived-in is authentic
```

## HK Body Type Realism

```yaml
HK_LOCAL_BODY_REALITY:
  - Petite to average height (155-165cm dominant)
  - Slim to athletic build (not extreme)
  - HK-specific body language: efficient movement, personal space awareness
  - Posture: slightly forward from phone use, but confident in own space
  - Walking: fast, purposeful — HK pace
  - Standing: weight shift from MTR practice, comfortable in crowd
  
WHAT ISN'T HK:
  ✗ Western body proportions (too tall, too curvy by HK standards)
  ✗ Japanese/Korean idol body type (too polished, too trained)
  ✗ Model-proportion body (reads as foreign model in HK)
```

# PART 3: HK BEHAVIORAL LANGUAGE

## HK-Specific Behaviors

```yaml
TRANSIT_BEHAVIOR:
  - Phone scrolling on MTR (universal HK behavior)
  - Standing with weight shift on moving train
  - Octopus card ready, bag positioned for easy access
  - Navigating crowd with spatial efficiency
  - Escalator — standing right, walking left
  - MTR platform — waiting in marked queue zones

FOOD_BEHAVIOR:
  - Eating while walking (HK pace)
  - Street food: egg waffle, fish ball, siu mai
  - Dai pai dong: plastic stool, shared table, casual
  - Milk tea/milk coffee — morning ritual
  - Late night noodles — post-drinking culture
  - Cha chaan teng: formica table, quick, efficient

SOCIAL_BEHAVIOR:
  - Friend groups in public: close but not overly physical
  - Phone sharing: showing each other screens
  - Photography: friend takes photo of friend (not selfie)
  - Laughing in public: muted but genuine
  - Personal space: smaller than Western, larger than mainland

WEATHER_BEHAVIOR:
  - Umbrella: sun umbrella in summer (UV protection)
  - Fan: handheld fan on hot days
  - Tissue packet: always in bag
  - AC-seeking: slightly sweaty entering cool space
  - Rain: quick shelter, not dramatic — HK rain is routine
  - Typhoon: wind-hair, leaning into wind, dramatic sky
```

# PART 5: HK MOOD & ATMOSPHERE

## Seasons & Weather Authenticity

```yaml
HK_SUMMER (May-September):
  → Hot + humid = sticky, glowing skin
  → Hair affected by humidity — slight frizz, flyaways
  → Sweat visible on skin (sheen, not dripping)
  → Light clothing, shorts, sandals
  → Umbrella for sun protection
  → Seeking shade, AC, cold drinks
  → Afternoon thunderstorm, sudden rain
  → Mood: energetic but slightly drained by heat

HK_WINTER (December-February):
  → Cool but not cold (10-18°C)
  → Light jacket, scarf, layers
  → No snow, no heavy coats
  → Outdoor dining comfortable
  → Hot drink culture: milk tea, hot lemon tea
  → Mood: energetic, social, outdoor-friendly

HK_SPRING (March-April):
  → Humid, foggy, damp
  → Light jacket, transitional clothing
  → Humidity haze in photos
  → Flowers: bauhinia, cotton tree
  → Mood: in-between, unpredictable

HK_AUTUMN (October-November):
  → Best weather — clear, dry, comfortable
  → Light layers, versatile outfits
  → Clear visibility, golden light
  → Outdoor activities peak
  → Mood: optimistic, active, social
```

# PART 10: DEEPSEEK V4 PRO VERIFICATION & EXTENSION
### Consolidation Date: 2026-06-02

## Verified Corrections

1. **Hair "NEVER perfect" softened:** Changed to "hair often shows humidity effects." Many HK women use anti-frizz products. The point is: perfect, motionless hair reads as AI-generated.

2. **Bauhinia season corrected:** Bauhinia (Hong Kong orchid tree) blooms in autumn/winter (October-December), not spring. Cotton tree (kapok) blooms in spring (March-April).

3. **MTR station colors clarified:** Not all stations have unique color schemes. MTR lines have standardized colors (Tsuen Wan=red, Kwun Tong=green). Some individual stations have unique tile colors but this is not universal.

4. **Neon signs updated:** Most traditional neon has been replaced by LED signs due to regulations. Added: "LED signs mimicking neon aesthetic" as modern reality.

5. **HK "never empty" softened:** Added qualifier: "HK is rarely completely empty, but early morning typhoon days, and certain residential areas can be quiet."

## Key Extensions Added

1. **HK Style Archetypes (SECTION ADDED):**
   - Central Office Girl: blazer+tank+wide trousers+sneakers, low bun, minimal makeup
   - Mong Kok Street Girl: oversized tee+shorts+chunky sneakers, claw clip, slight lip tint
   - Causeway Bay Shopper: crop top+high-waist+platform sneakers, hair down, more makeup
   - Shek O Beach Girl: bikini+shorts+sandals, messy bun, no makeup, tote bag

2. **HK Subcultures Added:** Hip-hop (LMF, indie scene), Art community (PMQ, Tai Kwun), LGBTQ+ (Pink Dot), local music (My Little Airport).

3. **HK Language Identity:** Written Cantonese (嘅, 咗, 唔) appears in signs, social media posts, text messages. This is a key visual authenticity marker.

4. **HK Queue Culture:** Strict queue etiquette (MTR, buses, restaurants). People queue in orderly lines — this is a behavioral signature.

5. **Sensory Signatures Added:** Each location type now has visual, audio, olfactory, tactile, and emotional signatures (MTR platform: train announcements in 3 languages; Wet market: red lamps, vendor calls, chopping sounds, incense smell).

6. **HK Transportation Beyond MTR:** Double-decker buses, trams (ding ding), minibuses (green/red), taxis (red/green), Star Ferry, outlying island ferries.

7. **HK Festival Behaviors:** Lunar New Year (red envelopes, flower markets), Mid-Autumn (mooncakes, lanterns), Dragon Boat (rice dumplings, races), Cheung Chau Bun Festival.

8. **HK Public Housing:** Distinctive architecture (Choi Hung rainbow estate, Shek Lei), open corridors, communal spaces, laundry hanging — iconic HK environments.

9. **Spectrum of Authenticity Framework:** Level 1 TOURIST_HK (skyline, Peak), Level 2 EXPAT_HK (LKF, Soho), Level 3 LOCAL_HK (MTR, market, dai pai dong), Level 4 DEEP_HK (public housing, wet market, local festival).

10. **Behavioral Motivation Framework:** EFFICIENCY (fast walking=HK pace, phone scrolling=transit optimization), SURVIVAL (umbrella=sun/rain, tissue=restroom necessity, power bank=long days), SOCIAL (friend photo=shared experience, muted laughter=public etiquette).
