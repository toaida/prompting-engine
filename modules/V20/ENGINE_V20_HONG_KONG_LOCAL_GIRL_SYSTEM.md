# ENGINE_V20_HONG_KONG_LOCAL_GIRL_SYSTEM

## Core Philosophy

HK local identity is not skyline wallpaper. It is behavior under density, humidity, transit, language, food, and social practicality.

```
Generic Asian city: pretty girl + signs.
HK local: style + object + behavior + place + weather all point to Hong Kong.
```

This module does not change lil.troublr canon. It localizes runtime scenes.

## Authenticity Spectrum

| Level | Token | Meaning |

Default for lil.troublr: `LOCAL_HK` with occasional `DEEP_HK` details.

## Behavioral Motivation Framework

HK behavior usually comes from:

### `EFFICIENCY`
- fast walking, direct movement
- phone scrolling on MTR to use time
- queue discipline
- eating practical walkable food only

### `SURVIVAL`
- umbrella for sun/rain
- tissue packet for public restroom / humidity / food
- power bank for long day
- water bottle / handheld fan in summer

### `SOCIAL`
- friend photos document shared experience
- phone sharing creates social body lean
- public laughter is genuine but not too loud
- personal space adapts to density

## HK Location Signatures

### MTR Platform
Visual: yellow safety line, station name in English + Traditional Chinese, crowd flow.
Behavior: phone gaze, queue, pole/strap awareness, bag held close.
Emotion: efficient, slightly tired, alert.

### Cha Chaan Teng
Visual: formica table, iced milk tea, receipt, tissue packet, tight booth.
Behavior: quick food, friend chat, phone on table, small social space.
Emotion: comfort, routine, local ease.

### Wet Market
Visual: red lamps, fish tanks, vegetable mist, plastic bags, narrow aisles.
Behavior: crouch/lean/inspect, careful stepping, bag in front.
Emotion: chaos, abundance, nostalgia.

### Public Housing / Estate
Visual: tiled walls, open corridors, laundry, benches, colorful facade, railings.
Behavior: waiting, phone, casual home-neighborhood ease.
Emotion: lived-in, local depth.

### HK Night Street
Visual: LED signs, wet pavement, taxi lights, convenience store glow.
Behavior: late food, waiting for taxi/MTR, social drink, umbrella.
Emotion: humid, cinematic, tired-social.

## Weather Rules

- Summer: humid hair effects, dewy skin, cold drinks, umbrella/fan.
- Winter: light jacket/scarf, hot drinks, no heavy snow coats.
- Spring: fog/mist, damp air, kapok/cotton tree blooms.
- Autumn: clear air, outdoor activity, better visibility.
- Bauhinia blooms autumn/winter, not spring.
- Traditional neon is rarer now; LED signs mimicking neon are modern HK reality.

---

## Language Authenticity

Use Traditional Chinese and Cantonese context where visible.

Examples:
- signs in Traditional Chinese + English
- WhatsApp-style message implication
- written Cantonese markers in props/signs if text appears: 嘅, 咗, 唔

Avoid simplified-Chinese mainland cues unless context specifically requires it.

---

## Anti-Patterns

- `GENERIC_ASIAN_CITY`: signs but no HK behavior.
- `TOURIST_SKYLINE_ONLY`: postcard view, no local life.
- `JAPAN_KOREA_STYLE_DRIFT`: too polished / idol-coded / Tokyo-coded.
- `EMPTY_HK_DEFAULT`: HK can be quiet sometimes, but default local spaces have density.
- `PERFECT_HUMIDITY_IGNORE`: hair/skin unaffected by HK weather.
- `MAINLAND_TRADITIONAL_DRIFT`: hanfu, simplified text, generic Chinese old-town aesthetic.
- `NO_OBJECT_SURVIVAL_KIT`: no umbrella/tissue/phone/bag in realistic day scenes.
